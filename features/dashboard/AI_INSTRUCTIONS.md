> **CRITICAL RULE FOR AI ASSISTANTS:**
> You are assigned to the **`feature/dashboard`** branch and the **`features/dashboard/`** folder.

### Allowed Actions:
- You may ONLY create, read, and modify files within `features/dashboard/`.
